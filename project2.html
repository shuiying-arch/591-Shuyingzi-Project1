San Diego Pothole Requests

July 1–September 30, 2026

This dashboard explores 2,694 pothole repair requests recorded in the City of San Diego's Get It Done dataset during the third quarter of 2026. It helps residents and other visitors explore where requests were reported and compare their recorded processing statuses.

Explore the dashboard

Click a status bar to filter the map and request count. Click the selected bar again to clear the selection. Click a map point to see its request details.

Open dashboard in a separate tab

Source: City of San Diego, Get It Done — Pothole Repair Requests. Records were selected by request date; this is a static dataset, not a live feed.

Interpretation: Points represent requests, not necessarily distinct potholes. “Closed” is a request status and does not by itself confirm a repair. Statuses reflect the downloaded data, not necessarily the status on September 30. Reporting patterns do not measure all road defects.
